# Section Library

Pick 6 to 8 sections. HERO and FINAL PUSH are mandatory. PROOF is mandatory
(real proof if provided, otherwise max 2 example reviews).
Order logic: Hook → Pain or Desire → Solution/Mechanism → Value proof
(features, demo, before/after) → Objections → Offer → Proof → Final push.

## Selection Guide

| Product type | Typical sections after HERO |
|--------------|-----------------------------|
| Problem solvers (cleaning, insoles, organizers, repair, privacy) | PROBLEM, SOLUTION, BEFORE/AFTER, FEATURES, HOW IT WORKS, OBJECTIONS |
| Beauty and care | PROBLEM or DESIRE, INGREDIENTS, HOW IT WORKS, WHAT TO EXPECT, BEFORE/AFTER |
| Clothing | DESIRE, SET CONTENTS/DETAILS, COLORS, SIZE GUIDE, OFFER |
| Perfume, jewelry, watches | DESIRE, DETAILS, GIFT, OFFER |
| Electronics, gadgets | PROBLEM or DESIRE, FEATURES, SPECS, BOX CONTENTS, HOW IT WORKS |
| Kids and baby | SAFETY, FEATURES, WHO IT IS FOR, GIFT |
| Kitchen and home | PROBLEM, FEATURES, HOW IT WORKS, BOX CONTENTS |
| Food and supplements | DESIRE or PROBLEM, INGREDIENTS, HOW IT WORKS, WHAT TO EXPECT |

Never include a section that does not fit (no before/after for perfume, no specs
for clothing, no ingredients for a gadget). Merge small sections to stay at 6-8.

## Blocks

Each block = one "Visual Flow" line + text lines with style in parentheses.
Write text lines one by one; headlines split into line 1 / line 2 when long.

**HERO** (mandatory, first 9:16 screen; rules in copy-rules.md → Above the Fold)
- Visual: result scene + product, text in empty space at top.
- Badge (small yellow): "جديد" or a real status.
- Headline line 1-2 (HUGE BOLD, deep accent color): dream outcome.
- Subhead (large dark): flexible angle.
- Mini proof: ★★★★★ + "[short quote]".
- Trust (large green): "📦 حلّ الكولي وشوف سلعتك قبل ما تخلص"

**PROBLEM** (pain products)
- Visual: muted, desaturated real-life scene of the problem, melting into the gradient.
- Headline (large bold red): the problem as the customer lives it.
- Body line 1-2: consequence on daily life.

**DESIRE** (desire products)
- Visual: aspirational scene in the real usage context.
- Headline: the feeling the customer wants. Body: the moment they will live.

**SOLUTION / MECHANISM**
- Visual: soft light burst, product appears in action.
- Headline: product as the answer, phrased as a result.
- Body: one line explaining WHY it works.

**BEFORE/AFTER**
- Visual: same subject from the same angle, two sides melting into each other,
  muted vs bright, no dividing line.
- Headline: the difference as a result. Before (red): "❌ قبل: …". After (green): "✅ بعد: …"

**FEATURES** (merge 2-4 features in one section)
- Visual: main product shot + small round close-up crops floating, no frames.
- Headline with content. Lines: "emoji + feature → benefit", short.

**SET CONTENTS / DETAILS** (clothing, kits)
- Visual: all pieces as cut-outs + one close-up crop.
- Headline: "طقم كامل من X قطع" style. One line per piece + gift line if any.

**COLORS**
- Visual: every variant photo side by side, exactly as uploaded, color dot under each.
- Headline with a reason to choose (tie to angle). Line: color names.

**SIZE GUIDE**
- Visual: simple silhouette icon floating, no table box.
- Label line + sizes line (HUGE). Fit note only if confirmed (elastic waist, weight range).

**HOW IT WORKS** (max 3-4 steps)
- Visual: numbered step images floating directly on the background, no boxes, no arrows.
- Headline with the result ("3 خطوات و…"). One short line per step.

**WHAT TO EXPECT** (only with confirmed timing)
- Visual: soft vertical timeline of glowing dots.
- Lines: "النهار الأول: …", "بعد أسبوع: …".

**INGREDIENTS / MATERIALS**
- Visual: product surrounded by floating ingredients/material close-ups.
- 3 lines: "ingredient → benefit".

**SPECS / BOX CONTENTS**
- Visual: product center with thin pointer lines, or flat-lay of the contents.
- Short lines, real numbers only.

**SAFETY** (kids, baby, skin)
- Visual: soft area with shield icon. Only confirmed facts.

**WHO IT IS FOR**
- Visual: 3 small floating portraits of real user types.
- Line: "type • type • type".

**GIFT**
- Visual: product with ribbon, soft bokeh. Headline: who to gift and when.

**OBJECTIONS** (top 3 doubts, from real Messenger questions when provided)
- Visual: 3 Q&A lines floating with small icons, no boxes.
- Headline with content ("سقسيتونا وجاوبناكم"). Q (bold) + A (one line) × 3.
- Always include the parcel answer to "واش يجي كيما في التصويرة؟".

**OFFER** (only with real form offers)
- Visual: product units/bundle floating with ribbon or sparkles, price next to it, never on it.
- Offer names and prices EXACTLY as in the form, one per line.
- Line explaining why the bigger offer (tied to angle). Gift line. Upsell seed line.

**PROOF**
- Real screenshots/photos provided → "wall": 3-6 screenshots floating, slightly
  rotated, no frames, headline quoting the best result.
- Otherwise: ★★★★★, headline quoting the customer's result, max 2 short reviews.

**FINAL PUSH** (mandatory, bottom where the gradient is deepest)
- Visual: product or model glowing on one side, text on the other. No block, no band.
- Urgency with a real reason, or skip urgency.
- Trust lines: parcel + 36-hour guarantee (white bold).
- Arrow line (HUGE, yellow or white): "⬇️ املأ الاستمارة تحت واختار عرضك"
- No CTA button (the form is right below).

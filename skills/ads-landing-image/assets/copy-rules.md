# Copy Rules: Arabic Direct Response for Long-Image Landing Pages

## Angles (STEP 1, question 1)

Propose 2 to 3 angles. Each angle in Arabic:
- **الجمهور**: who BUYS and who USES (often different: children buy for parents,
  sisters buy for a bride).
- **الدافع**: the single emotional motive.
- **مثال للعنوان**: one sample headline in darja.

Rules:
- Think about how the product is REALLY used in Algeria: at home or in public,
  for self or as a gift, which occasions, family customs. Never propose a usage
  that clashes with local culture (example: a crop top + mini skirt set is home
  wear for married women, never "for outings" or "the beach").
- Recommend one angle `[مقترح]` with a one-line reason.
- The angles double as ad tests: each one can become a separate Meta ad.
- The chosen angle controls everything. Never mix angles unless they share one motive.

Angle examples that worked:
| Product | Angle | Headline |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Floral home set | Married woman pampering herself | دلّلي روحك… راكي تستاهلي |
| Memory foam insole | Workers standing all day | واقف طول النهار؟ رجليك يستاهلو الراحة |
| Frosted glass paint | Privacy at home + shops | دارك ولا محلك مكشوف؟ |

## Above the Fold (first 9:16 screen of the image)

About 60% of visitors never scroll past it. Put 80% of the effort here.
1. **Headline = the customer's dream outcome**, never the product name or a
   description. Must be understood in 5 seconds.
2. **Subhead = flexible.** Pick the strongest for THIS product, never a fixed formula:
   how the result happens · speed (only if confirmed) · the feeling ·
   removing the main objection · who it is for.
3. **Visual shows the RESULT** (transformed state or happy user) with the product visible.
4. **Mini proof**: ★★★★★ + one short customer quote (real if provided).
5. **Trust line**: the parcel line from `store-profile.md`.
6. No CTA button here (the sticky bar does the job).

## Writing Rules (every line)

1. **Benefits over features.** Each feature is followed by what it changes in the
   customer's day. "ميموري فوم" → "تكمل نهارك بلا وجع".
2. **Scenes, not adjectives.** Describe a moment the customer recognizes. Banned
   without a concrete example: جودة عالية، ممتاز، مريح، تفاصيل مدروسة، رائع.
3. **Agitate the problem.** Problem → its consequence on daily life, family,
   money or self-image. "تسكّر الريدو وتقعد في الظلام نهار كامل".
4. **Mechanism in one simple line**: WHY it works.
5. **Answer the top 3 objections** on the page (quality vs photo, size/fit, ease
   of use, durability, value). Use the user's real Messenger questions first.
6. **Urgency needs a believable reason** (new batch, season, occasion). Never use
   "الكمية محدودة" alone. No reason → use the guarantee as the closing push instead.
7. **Talk like a person**, in darja, consistent from top to bottom.
8. **Vary headlines**: max 2 questions per page. Also use bold statements,
   specific results, "result + without effort" formulas.
9. **Never invent** numbers, prices, certifications, durations, results, or
   customer counts. Strong claim without data → ask in STEP 1 or drop it.

## Section Headlines

- A visitor reading ONLY headlines must understand the whole offer.
- Never generic: ❌ ثقة عملائنا ❌ كيف يعمل ❌ المميزات ❌ لون لكل مزاج (alone)
- Put the content in the headline: ✅ "قالولنا: رجلينا ولاو مرتاحين"
  ✅ "3 خطوات وطاقتك مغبّشة" ✅ "حلّ الكولي وشوف قبل ما تخلص"

## Offers and Upsell

- Repeat the form offers word for word, with prices. Explain WHY to take the
  bigger offer, tied to the angle ("وحدة للخدمة ووحدة للدار").
- Plant the upsell before the form: one natural line about needing a second
  unit or the complementary item.
- Gifts are framed as tied to the result ("الشوشو هدية من عندنا").

## Social Proof

- Real proof beats written proof: Messenger/WhatsApp screenshots, customer photos,
  real before/after. If provided, group them in one dense "wall" section and
  repeat one short quote in the first screen.
- No real proof → write at most 2 short realistic reviews matching the angle,
  mention the size/variant and a concrete detail. Flag them in the notes as
  examples to replace.

## Health, Medical and Sensitive Claims

- Never write "طبي", "يعالج", "يشفي", or promise health results without proof
  the user confirms. Use comfort/relief language: "يخفف الضغط", "تحس بالراحة".
- Before/after for body or health products must stay realistic and modest.
- These rules also protect Meta ads from rejection.
